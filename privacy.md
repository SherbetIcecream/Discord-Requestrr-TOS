Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this privately operated Requestrr Discord bot ("the Bot") processes information when you interact with it.

The Bot is operated for personal, non-commercial use and is not an official service of Discord or Requestrr.

1. Information Processed

When you interact with the Bot, information necessary to provide its functionality may be processed. Depending on the Bot's configuration, this may include:

Discord user identifiers
Discord server and channel identifiers
Commands and interactions sent to the Bot
Media titles and requests submitted through the Bot
Information necessary to associate requests with Discord users
Technical information necessary for operating, troubleshooting, and securing the Bot

The Bot is not intended to collect sensitive personal information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Bot is used solely to operate and maintain its functionality. This may include:

Processing media requests
Responding to Discord commands and interactions
Associating requests with the user who submitted them
Managing permissions and access
Troubleshooting errors
Preventing abuse or unauthorized use
Maintaining the security and reliability of the Bot

Information processed by the Bot is not used for advertising or user profiling.

3. Third-Party Services

The Bot may communicate with third-party services necessary to provide its functionality, including Discord, Requestrr, and configured media-management services.

Information may be transmitted to these services when necessary to process a command or media request.

Third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. The Bot operator does not control the privacy practices of those services.

4. Data Sharing

The Bot operator does not sell personal information.

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except when:

Necessary to operate the Bot or fulfill a user request
Processed by services connected to the Bot
Necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or connected systems
Required by applicable law or a valid legal request
5. Data Retention

Information may be retained by the Bot or connected services for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain request history, troubleshoot problems, or protect against abuse.

Retention periods may vary depending on the configuration and behavior of Requestrr, Discord, and other connected services.

Information that is no longer necessary may be deleted as part of normal maintenance.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the Bot and its configuration from unauthorized access.

However, no internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. Users should not submit passwords, authentication tokens, financial information, sensitive personal information, or other confidential data through Bot commands.

7. User Requests

Users may contact the Bot operator to ask questions about information associated with their use of the Bot or request deletion of information under the operator's control.

Requests may be made through the Discord server where the Bot is provided.

Information controlled independently by Discord or another third-party service may need to be managed directly through that service.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or Discord's requirements.

Users must satisfy Discord's applicable age requirements to use Discord and interact with the Bot.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the Bot, its configuration, connected services, or applicable requirements.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.

10. Contact

Questions, privacy inquiries, or requests regarding information processed by the Bot may be directed to the Bot operator through the Discord server where the Bot is provided.
